## Audit rules for continued work

- **Verified** means the finding is supported within the investigation's stated scope and is consistent with the current governing GUA-W records.
- **Verified** does not mean every sub-question is resolved, every analogy is an identity, or every possible application has been demonstrated.
- An unresolved question must remain explicitly identified; it must not be silently converted into a failure of the verified bounded finding.
- A later correction must identify the source, the exact claim changed, the evidence warranting the change, and the remaining limits.
- No AI investigation may redefine Alignment, Drift, the completed architecture, or the Author/Word hierarchy.
- Any material claim continues to be examined through the Triple Constraint Law.
