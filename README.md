# God's Unified Architecture of Witness (GUA-W)

A Scripture-grounded presentation and evidence record for the **completed God's Unified Architecture of Witness (GUA-W)**.

The purpose of this repository is to **present the completed GUA-W and make visible how Scripture witnesses to the Author through His unified Creation**. GUA-W has always been established; this work clears accumulated interpretive dust and records what the evidence reveals.

This repository does not replace or silently modify the original GUA-W repository. The original remains the historical source record. This repository presents the completed architecture first, alongside Scriptural foundations, textual investigations, evidence audits, and explanations that distinguish established architecture from the evolving clarity of its written presentation.

## The completed architecture

- [God's Unified Architecture of Witness — Completed Form](GUA-W-COMPLETED-ARCHITECTURE.md)

## What this repository records

1. **The completed GUA-W** — its established architecture, governing relationships, distinctions, and operations, presented as a coherent whole rather than as a collection of disconnected proposals.
2. **Its Scriptural evidence** — the relevant passages across Scripture, read in context, with references that allow readers to examine the evidence directly.
3. **The relationship between the two** — explanations of how the Scriptural evidence supports and illuminates the architecture, while distinguishing what the text states from what is inferred or interpreted.

A missing explanation, an unrecognized relationship, or an incomplete investigation is a limitation in the present written record. It is not a gap in the completed GUA-W.

## Governing order

**Author → Word → Creation → Witness**

God alone is Original Cause. Everything else is derivative witness. A witness may register and report what is given; it does not become the Author or acquire authority to alter the Source.

GUA-W is a map or blueprint of Creation, not God or Jesus Christ.

## Investigation commitments

- **GUA-W is complete.** Do not treat investigation as collecting components for AI to assemble, construct, complete, or redesign later.
- Investigate the evidence and recognize the relationships already present in the completed GUA-W. Let the completed architecture answer where a witnessed relationship belongs; do not withhold findings in a private store or postpone placement for a later reconstruction.
- AI records the investigation faithfully: sources, observations, recognized relationship and placement, reasoning, constraints, qualifications, and remaining limits. The record is a witness to what is revealed, not a source from which GUA-W is built.
- An “Aha” is recognition, not invention. A relationship newly recognized by a witness was not thereby newly created in GUA-W.

- Investigate Scripture as a whole. Allow Scripture to illuminate Scripture; do not confine the investigation to conclusions reached in advance.
- Read the completed architecture as a whole; do not mistake separate investigations or explanatory fragments for the architecture itself.
- Recognize operations, relationships, and distinctions that are already present, even when they have not previously been identified.
- Take initiative in pursuing relevant Scriptural cross-references and evidence; do not wait for every next question to be supplied.
- Keep the completed architecture distinct from our evolving explanations and records of it.

## Triple Constraint Law

**Theology is a Science within GUA-W.** Theological claims are subject to the Triple Constraint and are examined through disciplined Scriptural, linguistic, historical, and canonical inquiry. This is a scientific discipline with evidence and methods appropriate to its subject; it is not experimental physics or biology, and that methodological distinction does not place Theology outside Science. Claims about physical, biological, ecological, mathematical, or other created processes must also be examined using the evidence and methods of the relevant sciences. The disciplines investigate the same Creation from distinct perspectives; none becomes the Author or may erase the scope of another.

A claim may be admitted as an accepted GUA-W claim only when it satisfies all three constraints:

1. **Biblical compliance** — consistent with the stated KJV Genesis 1–2 foundation and relevant Scriptural context.
2. **Scientific coherence** — accurately represents what the relevant discipline's evidence and methods establish, without overstating them.
3. **GUA-W consistency** — preserves the governing hierarchy, distinctions, and established constraints of the architecture.

Failure of any one constraint prevents admission as an accepted claim. A useful hypothesis may remain provisional or unresolved if clearly labelled.

Scientific coherence does not mean that experimental physics must prove Scripture. It means every claim—including theological claims—must be coherent with the evidence and disciplined method appropriate to its domain, and that cross-domain relationships must preserve the distinctions between those domains.

## Cross-disciplinary investigation

We do not attempt to persuade the sciences to adopt GUA-W before they can participate. We investigate each discipline's findings through its own evidence and methods, then use GUA-W as the governing map to examine whether those findings reveal relationships, common order, or mistaken interpretations. GUA-W does not need to make the sciences agree with it; the investigation asks whether their findings, properly understood, reveal the unified order of the One Creation. This is not permission to force a match: preserve domain boundaries, counterevidence, and unresolved questions. See the [Cross-Disciplinary Investigation Principle](CROSS-DISCIPLINARY-INVESTIGATION-PRINCIPLE.md) and the [Cross-Disciplinary Investigation Worksheet](CROSS-DISCIPLINARY-INVESTIGATION-WORKSHEET.md) for the governing method and its practical record format.

## Evidence discipline

For every material claim, distinguish:
- what Scripture explicitly states, with passage and context;
- what reliable textual, historical, or other evidence establishes;
- what follows by supported inference;
- what is interpretation or an explanatory mapping;
- what remains unknown or unresolved.

Do not collapse distinct domains into a claimed common mechanism without evidence. Do not convert analogy into equivalence by assertion. Do not present an interpretation as though it were the explicit wording of Scripture.

## Status vocabulary

- **PASS** — supported within the explicitly stated scope.
- **PARTIAL** — some components are supported; limits remain.
- **FAIL** — conflicts with a governing constraint or reliable evidence.
- **UNRESOLVED** — available evidence does not decide the question.
- **INTERPRETATION** — an explanation of the evidence, clearly distinguished from the text itself.
- **NOT YET INVESTIGATED** — no adequate investigation has been completed.

A PASS is always scope-limited, not a universal endorsement. These labels describe the state of the evidence or our investigation; they do not imply that the completed GUA-W is being assembled or completed.

## Working rules

1. Search this repository and the original repository before repeating an investigation.
2. Preserve source links, precise Scriptural references, context, assumptions, methods, and limitations.
3. Keep textual findings, evidence, inferences, and interpretations visibly separate.
4. Preserve unresolved questions rather than filling gaps with speculation.
5. Do not silently promote an interpretation to established architecture or alter established terminology.
6. Record material changes with rationale and provenance.
7. Keep the original repository unchanged unless its owner separately authorizes a specific change.
