# Cross-Disciplinary Investigation Worksheet

**Status:** Working method for individual investigations; recorded 2026-11-17  
**Governing reference:** [Cross-Disciplinary Investigation Principle](CROSS-DISCIPLINARY-INVESTIGATION-PRINCIPLE.md)  
**Protocol:** [Investigation Protocol](INVESTIGATION-PROTOCOL.md)

## Purpose

This worksheet makes the governing principle usable in a concrete investigation. A discipline does not have to adopt GUA-W, its vocabulary, or its theological premises as a condition of having its findings examined. The investigation begins with the discipline's own question, evidence, and standards. GUA-W is then used as the governing map for examining relationships and interpretations.

This worksheet is a record-keeping aid, not a new GUA-W layer, Gear, runtime sequence, or claim that a correspondence exists before it is investigated.

## 1. Identify the disciplinary finding

- **Discipline and subfield:**
- **Question the discipline is addressing:**
- **Primary source(s):** Cite the paper, dataset, experiment, text, proof, or authoritative technical record.
- **Methods and conditions:**
- **What was directly observed, measured, or formally derived?**
- **What is the result, stated as narrowly as the source permits?**
- **Scope, assumptions, uncertainty, and known limitations:**

Do not begin by translating the result into GUA-W terminology. First establish what the source actually reports.

## 2. Separate result from interpretation

Record separately:

1. **Disciplinary result:** what the evidence or valid derivation establishes.
2. **Source authors' interpretation:** how the authors explain the result.
3. **Alternative interpretations:** credible competing accounts and their evidence.
4. **Our interpretation:** any explanation proposed during this investigation.

Do not treat a model, explanatory interpretation, or philosophical implication as though it were the direct observation itself.

## 3. Examine relationships across domains

Only after the disciplinary finding is accurately represented, ask:

- Does another discipline report a related observation, constraint, dependency, invariant, boundary, or process?
- Are the compared terms genuinely equivalent, merely analogous, or unrelated?
- Is there evidence for a shared mechanism, or only a formal/structural correspondence?
- Does the comparison preserve each discipline's scale, subject, definitions, and methods?
- What counterexample, alternative account, or new evidence could weaken or disconfirm the proposed relationship?

A relationship may be recognized without claiming that two domains are identical. Similar language is not sufficient evidence of a common mechanism.

## 4. Examine the relationship through the completed GUA-W

State the proposed relationship in ordinary language before assigning architectural terminology.

- **What relationship is being recognized?**
- **Which evidence supports it?**
- **Where does it belong in the completed GUA-W, if a precise placement is supported?**
- **Why is that placement warranted by the completed architecture?**
- **What distinction must remain intact?**
- **What is not established by this comparison?**

If precise placement is not supported, record the relationship and the exact limitation. Do not invent a new component or call the completed architecture incomplete.

## 5. Apply the Triple Constraint Law

Assess each material claim, not merely the investigation as a whole.

| Constraint | Questions |
|---|---|
| **Biblical compliance** | Does the claim remain consistent with the KJV Genesis 1–2 foundation and relevant Scripture read in context? Does it distinguish explicit text from inference and interpretation? |
| **Scientific coherence** | Does it accurately represent the source evidence, methods, definitions, assumptions, scope, uncertainty, and counterevidence of the relevant discipline(s)? |
| **GUA-W consistency** | Does it preserve the Author → Word → Creation → Witness hierarchy and the distinctions and relationships of the completed architecture? |

**Disposition:** PASS (within stated scope) / PARTIAL / FAIL / UNRESOLVED / INTERPRETATION.

A claim that fails a constraint is not admitted as an accepted GUA-W claim. A partial or unresolved result is retained with its limits rather than forced into agreement.

## 6. Record the outcome

Choose the description that matches the evidence:

- **Supported relationship:** evidence warrants the stated relationship within a defined scope.
- **Limited correspondence or analogy:** a useful comparison exists, but identity or common mechanism is not established.
- **Mistaken interpretation identified:** a particular interpretation fails against source evidence, method, or scope. State exactly which interpretation; do not dismiss an entire discipline.
- **No relationship established:** the investigation found no adequate basis for the proposed comparison.
- **Unresolved:** evidence is incomplete, conflicting, or insufficient to decide.

Include source citations, counterevidence, qualifications, and the next discriminating question. Record findings in the appropriate subject-matter file and update its register/navigation when needed. Do not leave a recognized finding in private notes for later assembly.

## 7. Quality-control questions before publication

- Have we represented the source's actual finding fairly?
- Have we distinguished data or derivation from interpretation?
- Have we considered credible alternatives and counterevidence?
- Have we avoided requiring a discipline to adopt GUA-W terminology?
- Have we avoided changing the discipline's data, equations, definitions, or conclusions to create a match?
- Have we avoided claiming scientific confirmation of GUA-W where only a bounded correspondence is supported?
- Have we applied all three constraints to each material claim?
- Have we recorded what remains unknown?
- Have we recognized a supported relationship in the completed architecture without adding or redesigning an architectural component?

## Governing reminder

**The discipline supplies its evidence and the standards by which its findings are established. GUA-W supplies the governing map for examining the relationship of those findings to the unified order of the One Creation. Neither role should be confused with the other.**

The aim is not to make separate sciences speak with one vocabulary. It is to investigate whether their properly understood findings witness to relationships and common order in the One Creation.
