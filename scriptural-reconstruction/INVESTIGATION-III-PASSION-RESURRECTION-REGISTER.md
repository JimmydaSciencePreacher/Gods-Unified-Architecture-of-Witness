## Purpose and boundary

Investigation II recorded thirty-five event-level studies of miracles and events of and surrounding Jesus. The Resurrection is not being added as an ordinary thirty-sixth miracle. It receives a separate central investigation because of its unique place in the canonical testimony concerning Jesus Christ, the Word made flesh, the last Adam, and the firstfruits of them that slept.

This register tracks the related Passion and post-Resurrection events so that they are not collapsed into one undifferentiated account. The Resurrection remains central; the surrounding signs and appearances retain their own Scriptural details.

**Governing order:** Author → Word → Creation → Witness.

GUA-W is complete. These investigations recognize and record evidence in relation to the completed architecture; they do not build, complete, or redesign it. Jesus Christ is not a component of GUA-W.

## Governing distinctions

1. The death and burial of Jesus must be examined as the events preceding the Resurrection proclamation, not presumed away.
2. The darkness, torn veil, earthquake, and opened graves are distinct reports. Their timing, wording, and scope must be examined separately.
3. The empty tomb is not identical to the full Resurrection claim. The Gospel accounts also report appearances of the risen Jesus.
4. The appearances must be compared account by account. Differences in named witnesses, locations, sequence, and details must be preserved before any harmonized chronology is proposed.
5. The Ascension is a distinct event and must not be merged with the Resurrection or with the appearances during the forty days.
6. The Gospel writers' wording and the KJV text remain primary for this record. Textual-critical questions, including Mark 16:9–20, must be explicitly identified and investigated.
7. Claims about physical or biological processes require evidence and methods appropriate to those disciplines. No unreported mechanism may be inserted into Scripture.
8. Theological, historical, textual, and scientific claims all remain subject to the Triple Constraint Law.

## Triple Constraint

- **Biblical compliance:** preserve the KJV wording and each passage's context; compare Scripture with Scripture; distinguish explicit statements from inference.
- **Scientific coherence:** use methods and evidence appropriate to historical, medical, physical, and other claims; do not overstate what a method can establish.
- **GUA-W consistency:** preserve God as Original Cause, the unique identity of Jesus Christ, the completed architecture, and the derivative role of witnesses.
