# Chapter 17 — Scientific Evidence Audits and Open Questions

## From a broad comparison to a claim-by-claim examination

Chapter 16 established the way the sciences are approached: each discipline is allowed to speak through its own evidence, definitions, methods, and limits. The unity of the One Creation does not make every scientific claim equally certain, and it does not make every proposed relationship a demonstrated correspondence.

This chapter moves from that governing approach to the repository's actual scientific evidence audits.

An audit is not a search for a scientific phrase that resembles a GUA-W term. It asks a more exact question:

**What does the source evidence establish, what has been inferred from it, what has been added by interpretation, and what remains unresolved?**

The distinction matters because a source can contain a sound scientific result and an unsound conclusion drawn from it. The result should not be discarded merely because the interpretation overreaches. Nor should the interpretation inherit the authority of the result merely because both appear in the same paper or historical trace.

The governing order remains:

**Author → Word → Creation → Witness**

The scientist's paper, the mathematical model, the experimental record, and this chapter are all derivative witnesses. They may be careful, useful, and reliable within a stated scope. None becomes the Author or the source of the reality being investigated.

## The evidence has to be separated before it can be evaluated

A useful evidence audit distinguishes at least six kinds of statement.

1. **Observed or measured result.** What was detected, recorded, or measured? Under what conditions, with what apparatus, and with what uncertainty?
2. **Formal result.** What follows mathematically from stated definitions, axioms, and assumptions?
3. **Model-dependent result.** What follows within a specified theoretical framework, approximation, or idealization?
4. **Interpretation.** What account is offered for what the result means? Does the evidence require that interpretation, or are alternatives still available?
5. **Proposed extension.** What additional claim, mechanism, experiment, or generalization is being suggested?
6. **Open question.** What has not yet been decided by the evidence examined?

These categories are not a ranking in which mathematical or theoretical work is automatically inferior to experiment. They identify what kind of support a statement has. A rigorous theorem can establish a precise formal result. An experiment can establish a reproducible observation. Neither should be made to establish a different kind of claim without the additional reasoning required.

A source audit therefore records both the strength of a result and the boundary of its reach.

## Case study 1 — Quantum foundations and unitarity

The quantum foundations audit begins with several sound elements of ordinary quantum mechanics.

For a pure state in non-relativistic quantum mechanics, a normalized vector in a complex Hilbert space is a standard representation. More general statistical mixtures require density operators. For a closed system governed by a self-adjoint Hamiltonian, Schrödinger evolution is unitary. Under that evolution, inner products and norms are preserved; for a density operator evolving unitarily, its eigenvalues and von Neumann entropy are preserved.

Those statements have defined mathematical conditions. They do not say that every subsystem interacting with an environment has unchanged entropy, or that every practical description of an open system is itself unitary. The system under consideration must be specified.

The audit also examines the no-cloning theorem. Standard quantum mechanics does not permit one universal physical operation to perfectly clone every possible arbitrary unknown quantum state. That is a precise result. It does not prohibit copying classical information, preparing additional systems in known states, or copying mutually orthogonal states under appropriate conditions.

The historical trace made a larger move: it treated unitarity and no-cloning as scientific verification of a universal GUA-W axiom about information never being retroactively modified, deleted, or rewritten. The audit does not admit that conclusion. The mathematical results and the proposed epistemic rule are different claims. A comparison may be useful as an analogy about preserving distinctions, but a mathematical identity or universal physical law has not been established.

**What is retained:** unitary evolution for the specified closed-system formalism; the associated preservation results; and the no-cloning theorem within its scope.

**What is not established:** that these results prove a universal prohibition on information deletion, prove every subsystem preserves entropy, or scientifically verify a GUA-W axiom.

**Open question:** Is there a carefully defined, limited correspondence between a particular information-preserving transformation in quantum theory and a particular GUA-W distinction? If so, the comparison must name the mathematical object, the architectural relationship, and the exact limit. The word *information* alone is not enough.

## Case study 2 — The double-slit experiment and the observer

The double-slit experiment demonstrates interference when coherent contributions from the available paths remain capable of interfering. When a physical interaction correlates path alternatives with a detector or environment so that which-path information becomes available, interference in the relevant observed distribution is suppressed.

This account must be stated carefully. The experiment does not require us to picture a classical particle splitting into two ordinary pieces of matter. Nor does the presence of a detector make the particle a classical solid object in every respect. The quantum description continues to apply.

Decoherence explains an important part of the process. Interactions with apparatus and environmental degrees of freedom can correlate the alternatives; when those environmental degrees of freedom are not tracked, the reduced description exhibits suppression of interference. This helps explain the emergence of stable, classical-looking records.

But decoherence alone does not settle every aspect of the measurement problem or explain, by itself, why one particular outcome is experienced. That boundary must remain visible.

The word *observer* also requires discipline. In quantum physics it may refer to a measurement arrangement, detector, or physical interaction. It does not automatically mean a conscious human being. A blanket claim that standard quantum mechanics says human consciousness creates physical reality is not supported by the experiment or by the range of standard measurement treatments.

The original trace proposed a GUA-W-style data-handoff description and a possible gravitational which-path test. The audit keeps those proposals separate from established results. A metaphor about a detector producing a record is not a derivation of measurement. A proposed gravitational test needs a complete physical model, apparatus, coupling, predicted observable, competing explanations, noise budget, and feasibility analysis before it can be treated as a testable experiment.

**What is retained:** interference, the effect of which-path information, physical correlations with apparatus and environment, and decoherence's role in suppressing interference in reduced descriptions.

**What is not established:** a literal classical-fluid picture of the particle, consciousness as a universal cause of measurement outcomes, or a GUA-W data-routing mechanism as the physical explanation.

**Open questions:** How should the remaining measurement problem be characterized within the chosen interpretation? Can the proposed gravitational which-path experiment be formulated quantitatively and shown to distinguish competing predictions? What precise, limited relationship—if any—can be recognized between physical measurement records and the GUA-W language of witness or registration?

## Case study 3 — Entanglement and ER=EPR

Quantum entanglement is an established feature of quantum theory. Bell-test experiments have demonstrated correlations that violate Bell inequalities. Those correlations do not permit controllable faster-than-light messaging.

Einstein-Rosen bridge geometries are mathematical structures admitted in general relativity. The ER=EPR conjecture proposes a deep relationship between entanglement and wormhole geometry in particular theoretical settings, especially in work involving holographic models.

The distinction between these statements is essential. The existence of entanglement does not establish that every entangled pair is joined by a literal microscopic wormhole. The conjecture does not establish that separating particles stretches a physical wormhole, stores energy in it, or that breaking entanglement universally tears spacetime. Those stronger claims require evidence and a defined model that the conjecture's name cannot supply.

The GUA-W comparison also needs more precision than the shared word *joining*. Joining might refer to connection, interaction, common origin, correlation, or a shared-state description. These are not synonyms. A valid comparison must identify which relation is under examination and whether the comparison is empirical, mathematical, causal, structural, or analogical.

**What is retained:** entanglement, Bell-inequality-violating correlations, the no-signalling boundary, Einstein-Rosen bridge solutions, and ER=EPR as a conjectural relationship in specified settings.

**What is not established:** a universal literal wormhole for every entangled pair, instantaneous communication, or a universal mechanism by which disentanglement destroys spacetime.

**Open questions:** Which precise feature of entanglement is relevant to the proposed GUA-W comparison? What theoretical model could test that comparison? What would count as evidence against it? Until those questions are answered, the correspondence remains a question for investigation, not an established placement or mechanism.

## Case study 4 — Holography and black-hole information

Black-hole thermodynamics gives a major result: the Bekenstein–Hawking entropy formula relates black-hole entropy to horizon area. This area relation helped motivate the holographic principle.

The AdS/CFT correspondence provides a powerful and precise example of holographic duality in specified theoretical settings. It relates a gravitational bulk description to a lower-dimensional non-gravitational quantum field theory. The correspondence is not simply a claim that every ordinary three-dimensional volume is literally projected onto a two-dimensional screen. Nor does the AdS/CFT example establish a universal description of every spacetime or of our observed universe.

The audit also separates quantities that the historical trace treated too freely as interchangeable: black-hole thermodynamic entropy, boundary-state von Neumann entropy, entanglement entropy of a boundary subregion, and generalized entropy. Their relations depend on the specified state, region, duality, approximation, and formula.

Page-curve reasoning and modern calculations using replica wormholes and quantum extremal surfaces are important developments in controlled theoretical models. For an initially pure system undergoing unitary evaporation, the entropy of the emitted radiation is expected to rise and later fall toward zero if the complete final radiation state is pure. But the Page time is not universally a literal halfway point in elapsed evaporation time, and the curve need not be geometrically symmetric. A theoretical calculation is not the same thing as a direct observation of information emerging from an astrophysical black hole.

**What is retained:** black-hole area entropy; the significance of holographic dualities in their specified domains; the relationship between boundary unitarity and information preservation within an appropriate duality; and the importance of Page-curve and quantum-extremal-surface calculations.

**What is not established:** universal dimensional reduction for every physical region, a literal information screen on every horizon, or a complete empirical account of every astrophysical black hole.

**Open questions:** How far can results from controlled holographic models be extended to other spacetimes? Which entropy is being compared in a given claim? What physical or mathematical conditions are required for the proposed correspondence? Which parts of the black-hole information problem remain open beyond the results of a particular model?

## A cross-case pattern: a strong result can sit beside an overextended conclusion

Across these four audits, a recurring pattern appears.

- A formal result is extended beyond its assumptions.
- A phenomenon is given one interpretation as though no alternatives exist.
- A conjecture is described as an established identity.
- A result in a controlled theoretical setting is generalized to every physical setting.
- A technical term is transferred between disciplines without checking whether its meaning remains the same.
- A useful analogy is promoted into a causal mechanism without an independent derivation.

The remedy is not to reject the underlying science. It is to retain the supported result and correct the claim attached to it.

This is why each audit needs claim-level dispositions. An overall label such as PARTIAL does not mean that every sentence in a source is partly true. Some claims may PASS within scope, others may FAIL, and still others may remain PROPOSED or UNRESOLVED. The disposition belongs to the individual claim, not merely to the document as a whole.

## What an evidence audit can and cannot decide

A scientific audit can determine whether a scientific statement accurately represents the relevant formalism, experiment, model, or source. It can identify unsupported generalizations, missing assumptions, mistaken terminology, and competing explanations.

A scientific audit by itself cannot decide whether a theological interpretation complies with Scripture. That requires the relevant Scriptural and canonical examination. Likewise, a theological comparison cannot substitute for the technical analysis needed to establish a physical mechanism.

The Triple Constraint remains inseparable:

### Biblical compliance

The relevant Scriptural text and context must be examined directly. A scientific analogy must not be presented as though Genesis explicitly states it. A physics result does not determine theological meaning.

### Scientific coherence

The claim must accurately represent its discipline's evidence, definitions, assumptions, model, uncertainty, and limits. Theoretical, experimental, mathematical, and interpretive claims must be identified as such.

### GUA-W consistency

The claim must preserve the completed architecture and the governing order. A scientific witness may reveal a relationship worth recognizing; it does not become the Source, and its vocabulary does not authorize an alteration of GUA-W.

A claim that fails one of these constraints is not admitted as an accepted GUA-W claim. A useful proposal may remain recorded as proposed or unresolved, with its status made explicit.

## Open questions are part of an honest record

An unresolved question is not a defect to be hidden, and it is not permission to fill the space with speculation. It is a precise statement about the present reach of the investigation.

The scientific audits leave questions of several different kinds:

- **Empirical:** Is there an observation or experiment that could distinguish the proposed explanations?
- **Mathematical:** Does the proposed relationship follow from a formal derivation, and under what assumptions?
- **Model-dependent:** Does the result hold only in a particular model, or is there a justified path to a broader domain?
- **Interpretive:** Do the same results permit more than one interpretation?
- **Cross-disciplinary:** Are the compared concepts genuinely related, or do they merely share a word?
- **Architectural:** Does the evidence reveal a relationship already recognizable within the completed GUA-W, or is the proposed mapping only an analogy?

These questions should not be merged into one generic category called “unknown.” Each requires a different kind of investigation and a different kind of evidence.

The repository's rejected and unresolved register is therefore not an appendix of embarrassment. It is part of the evidence record. It allows the reader to distinguish what has been established, what has been rejected, what remains under examination, and what has not yet been investigated.

## The place of AI in the audit

AI can assist with locating sources, comparing claims, tracing citations, organizing evidence, identifying contradictions, and drafting a readable account of the findings. It can also make mistakes: it may misread a source, confuse a conjecture with a result, or produce a confident explanation that the evidence does not support.

Its output is derivative. A generated summary is not the primary source. A citation must be checked. A proposed correspondence must be tested. An AI-generated sentence does not become evidence by appearing in a polished chapter.

The same standard applies to this chapter. Its purpose is to direct the reader to the underlying audits and primary-source trails, not to replace them. Where the technical detail matters, the reader should follow the research links and examine the claim in its original setting.

## Conclusion — the witness must not say more than it has seen

The scientific audits do not diminish the sciences. They protect the value of their findings by refusing to make them carry conclusions they have not established.

Quantum unitarity remains a precise result within its assumptions. The double-slit experiment remains a powerful demonstration of interference and the physical significance of which-path information. Entanglement and Bell-inequality violations remain established findings. ER=EPR remains a conjecture in particular theoretical settings. Black-hole area entropy and holographic dualities remain important results whose scope must be stated accurately.

The larger lesson is consistent across the cases: evidence, interpretation, analogy, and speculation must not be confused. A scientific witness is most useful when it reports faithfully, including the boundary of what it knows.

The sciences investigate the One Creation through differentiated methods. Theology addresses the ultimate WHY of that Creation; the other sciences investigate its operations and legitimate proximate questions. Neither is served by forcing a match or hiding a genuine unresolved question.

**A witness is not made more reliable by saying more than the evidence permits.**

**One Author. One originating Word. One Creation. Many witnesses.**
