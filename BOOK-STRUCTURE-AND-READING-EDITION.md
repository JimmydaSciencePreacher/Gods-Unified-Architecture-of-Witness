# God's Unified Architecture of Witness
## Book Structure and Reading Edition

**Purpose:** Present the existing GUA-W record as a coherent book-length reading journey while preserving the complete research library as its evidence base.

**Editorial status:** Reading architecture established; continuous chapter prose remains an editorial work in progress.

**Governing order:** Author → Word → Creation → Witness.

> One Author. One originating Word. One Creation. Many witnesses.

## How this edition works

This is the reader's route through the repository. It is not a replacement for the source investigations, does not rewrite their findings, and does not create or alter GUA-W. Each chapter introduces a subject in a comprehensible order and directs the reader to the primary research documents that support it.

The repository has two complementary layers:

1. **The Book** — a continuous explanation, arranged in parts and chapters, for readers who want to understand the whole without first navigating dozens of separate files.
2. **The Research Library** — the detailed textual reconstructions, language audits, canonical investigations, scientific evidence audits, registers, and unresolved questions that allow every claim to be checked.

Read the chapters in sequence for the main argument. Follow the linked investigations when you want the underlying evidence. A source file may support more than one chapter; that is intentional because one investigation can illuminate several parts of the unified account.

## Editorial rules for developing the continuous book

1. **Do not rewrite the evidence into a simplified claim that changes its meaning.** The chapter explains; the source investigation remains available for verification.
2. **Keep Scripture first.** Present the passage and its context before the architectural relationship or theological interpretation.
3. **Distinguish text, observation, inference, interpretation, and unresolved limits.**
4. **Preserve the Triple Constraint Law.** No chapter may admit a claim that fails any constraint.
5. **Do not manufacture chronology.** Where Scripture does not state a sequence or date, the chapter must say so and identify the relevant passages that bear on the question.
6. **Do not create GUA-W components.** New understanding may clarify the written presentation; it does not redesign the completed architecture.
7. **Use one canonical source investigation per subject.** Other chapters cross-reference it rather than duplicating it.
8. **Preserve provenance.** Link every substantive claim to its supporting investigation or evidence audit.
9. **Keep the main narrative readable.** Move detailed word studies, exhaustive verse tables, technical methods, and audit trails to the research library or appendices.
10. **Make unresolved questions visible.** Do not conceal them in footnotes or imply that an unfinished investigation is complete.
