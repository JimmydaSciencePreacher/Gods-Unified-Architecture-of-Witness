# Cross-Disciplinary Investigation Principle

**Study status:** Governing methodological principle; recorded 2026-11-17  
**Scope:** Investigation of findings across the sciences through the completed GUA-W  
**Relationship:** Applies the Triple Constraint Law and the existing Author → Word → Creation → Witness hierarchy. It does not add a layer, Gear, or component to GUA-W.

## Governing statement

We do not attempt to persuade the sciences to adopt GUA-W before they can participate in an investigation. We use GUA-W as the governing map through which we examine the findings of each discipline and recognize where their evidence already reveals relationships, common order, or mistaken interpretations.

**GUA-W does not need to make the sciences agree with it. It needs to investigate whether their findings, properly understood, reveal the unified order of the One Creation.**

**Theology is the Queen of the Sciences** in the specific sense that it addresses the ultimate WHY of the One Creation—its Author, originating Word, purpose, and end. The other sciences investigate HOW the created order operates within their respective domains. Their findings remain necessary and authoritative within their proper scope; they do not, by describing mechanisms, independently settle the ultimate WHY. This primacy does not authorize Theology to invent or dictate domain-specific mechanisms. See [Theology: The Queen of the Sciences](THEOLOGY-QUEEN-OF-THE-SCIENCES.md) for the full distinction.

This is an investigative orientation, not a demand that evidence reach a predetermined result. The map governs the questions of relationship, order, and architectural consistency; it does not replace the methods, data, equations, standards of measurement, or domain-specific expertise by which a discipline establishes its findings.

## What this means in practice

1. **Enter through the discipline's evidence.** Identify what researchers measured, observed, derived, modeled, or inferred. Read the finding in its original disciplinary context.
2. **Respect domain and method.** Use the standards appropriate to the field. Do not require physics to use theological methods, theology to imitate laboratory physics, or one discipline to surrender its proper subject matter to another.
3. **Separate finding from interpretation.** Record the result, the authors' interpretation, competing interpretations, assumptions, uncertainty, and limits distinctly.
4. **Examine the map-level relationship.** Ask whether the evidence, properly understood, reveals a relationship, common order, boundary, dependency, or distinction that can be recognized within the completed GUA-W.
5. **Test mistaken interpretations in both directions.** A scientific interpretation may exceed its evidence; a GUA-W interpretation may also misread or overstate a scientific result. Neither is protected from correction by assertion.
6. **Apply the Triple Constraint to claims.** Biblical compliance, scientific coherence, and GUA-W consistency remain inseparable. Scientific coherence means faithful treatment of the relevant evidence and method—not a requirement that every discipline produce identical explanations or that science experimentally prove Scripture.
7. **Preserve nonmatches and open questions.** If no relationship is established, say so. If evidence is incomplete, conflicting, or outside the available scope, retain the question as unresolved. Do not manufacture agreement, force an analogy into equivalence, or call a mismatch a scientific failure.
8. **Record relationships as they are recognized.** Cite sources, state the evidentiary basis, locate the relationship where supported in the completed architecture, and preserve qualifications. Do not collect findings privately for later construction or redesign.

## Guardrails against confirmation bias

- GUA-W is the governing map for this project's interpretation of the One Creation; it is not a substitute for inspecting source evidence.
- A result is not accepted merely because it sounds compatible with GUA-W.
- A result is not rejected merely because a current interpretation appears incompatible. First examine whether the interpretation, assumptions, scope, or translation of the result is mistaken.
- Do not alter scientific data, equations, definitions, or disciplinary conclusions to obtain architectural agreement.
- Do not represent a proposed correspondence as an established scientific mechanism.
- Do not claim that a discipline has independently confirmed GUA-W when the evidence supports only a limited correspondence or analogy.
- Retain counterevidence, alternative explanations, and unresolved distinctions in the record.

## Investigation sequence

**Discipline's question → source evidence and method → result and scope → interpretations and alternatives → Triple Constraint examination → relationship (if supported) within GUA-W → recorded limits and next question.**

This sequence is a research procedure, not a new runtime sequence or architecture layer.

## Statement types to preserve

| Type | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Disciplinary finding | What the field's evidence or valid derivation establishes within stated scope |
| Disciplinary interpretation | An explanation of the finding; may be contested |
| Cross-disciplinary relationship | A correspondence or dependency supported by evidence across domains |
| GUA-W recognition | The placement or significance of that relationship within the completed map |
| Analogy | A limited comparison, not identity or shared mechanism |
| Mistaken interpretation | A reading that fails against source evidence, method, or scope |
| Unresolved | Evidence does not yet decide the issue |

## Present disposition

This principle is a governing method for future cross-disciplinary work. It does not itself establish any particular scientific correspondence or settle any scientific dispute. Each proposed relationship must be investigated individually and recorded with its sources, scope, counterevidence, and Triple Constraint assessment.

**Core reminder:** We are not recruiting sciences to endorse GUA-W. We are examining what their evidence witnesses about the One Creation, while allowing the evidence to correct our understanding of what has been observed.
