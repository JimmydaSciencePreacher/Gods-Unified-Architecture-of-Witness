# Primary-Source Verification — ER=EPR and Quantum Entanglement

**Review date:** 2026-11-17  
**Parent record:** [Evidence Audit — ER=EPR and Quantum Entanglement](EVIDENCE-AUDIT-ER-EPR-and-Quantum-Entanglement.md)  
**Historical source:** Original Diagnostic Case Trace 003, the earlier diagnostic record from which this evidence audit developed.  
**Scope:** Verify the key distinctions between experimental Bell correlations, Einstein-Rosen bridge solutions, the ER=EPR proposal, and claims about generic entangled systems. This is a targeted literature audit, not a derivation-level review of every ER=EPR model.

## 1. Executive assessment

**Status: PARTIAL.** The existing evidence audit has the right overall boundary: quantum entanglement and Bell-inequality violations are experimentally established; general relativity contains nontraversable Einstein-Rosen bridge solutions; ER=EPR is a conjectural theoretical proposal whose strongest formulations concern particular entangled black-hole states and gravitational/holographic settings. The primary sources do not establish that every generic entangled pair has a literal smooth wormhole in ordinary spacetime.

An important distinction is sharpened here: the original Maldacena-Susskind paper does not state that entanglement and wormholes are simply the same phenomenon for every pair of particles. It begins with a specific connection between maximally entangled black holes and a two-sided bridge, then suggests that similar bridges might be present for more general entangled states. That extension is a conjectural proposal, not an experimentally verified universal identity.

## 2. Claim E-01 — Bell correlations and experimental status

**Primary sources**
- John S. Bell, “On the Einstein Podolsky Rosen Paradox,” *Physics Physique Fizika* 1, 195–200 (1964): https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysicsPhysiqueFizika.1.195
- Bas Hensen et al., “Loophole-free Bell inequality violation using electron spins separated by 1.3 kilometres,” *Nature* 526, 682–686 (2015): https://doi.org/10.1038/nature15759
- Marissa Giustina et al., “Significant-loophole-free test of Bell's theorem with entangled photons,” *Physical Review Letters* 115, 250401 (2015): https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.115.250401
- Lynden K. Shalm et al., “A strong loophole-free test of local realism,” *Physical Review Letters* 115, 250402 (2015): https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.115.250402

Bell's theorem derives constraints on correlations if the relevant local-hidden-variable assumptions hold. The 2015 experiments reported Bell-inequality violations while addressing major experimental loopholes. These are experimental findings about statistical correlations and the failure of broad local-realist models under the experiments' assumptions.

**Boundary:** Bell-inequality violation is not a demonstration of controllable faster-than-light messaging. Quantum correlations do not allow one party to choose an outcome and use it to transmit a controllable message to the distant party. “Nonlocal correlations” must not be paraphrased as a usable superluminal signal or as a measured wormhole.

**Disposition:** PASS for the existence of entanglement and Bell-inequality violations in tested systems; FAIL for describing the results as evidence that an actual wormhole was observed.

## 3. Claim E-02 — Einstein-Rosen bridge geometry

**Primary source:** Albert Einstein and Nathan Rosen, “The Particle Problem in the General Theory of Relativity,” *Physical Review* 48, 73–77 (1935): https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRev.48.73

General relativity admits the mathematical bridge construction associated with the maximally extended Schwarzschild solution. The standard Einstein-Rosen bridge is not a traversable channel through which an observer can send a signal from one exterior region to the other. This is a statement about a particular spacetime geometry and its causal structure, not an observation of a bridge connecting ordinary entangled laboratory particles.

**Disposition:** PASS as a mathematical/geometric statement with causal qualification.

## 4. Claim E-03 — What Maldacena and Susskind actually proposed

**Primary source:** Juan Maldacena and Leonard Susskind, “Cool horizons for entangled black holes,” *Fortschritte der Physik* 61 (2013) 781–811: https://arxiv.org/abs/1306.0533

The abstract states that general relativity contains solutions in which two distant black holes are connected through the interior by an Einstein-Rosen bridge. Those solutions can be interpreted as maximally entangled states of two black holes forming a complex EPR pair. The authors then suggest that similar bridges might be present for more general entangled states.

**Interpretive boundary:** The first statement concerns a specific black-hole geometry/state relation. The extension to more general entangled states is explicitly framed as a suggestion. The paper does not report an experiment observing wormholes between generic entangled particles. The slogan “ER = EPR” must not erase the model, state, and gravitational assumptions.

**Disposition:** PASS as a named conjectural research proposal; UNRESOLVED as a universal physical identity for arbitrary entangled pairs.

## 5. Claim E-04 — Can a generic wormhole be detected as an entanglement witness?

**Primary source:** Ning Bao, Jason Pollack, and Grant N. Remmen, “Wormhole and Entanglement (Non-)Detection in the ER=EPR Correspondence,” *Journal of High Energy Physics* 11 (2015) 126: https://arxiv.org/abs/1509.05426

The authors examine whether spacetime observables could unambiguously identify a generic entangled state by detecting a corresponding wormhole geometry. They argue that no measurement in general relativity unambiguously detects the presence of a generic wormhole geometry, in a result they describe as dual to the undetectability of generic entanglement.

**What this means:** The paper is not experimental evidence that generic wormholes exist. It analyzes a consistency issue for the proposed correspondence and shows why a naive “look for a wormhole to detect entanglement” test is not available in the generic case.

**Disposition:** PASS as a theoretical limitation relevant to ER=EPR; it reinforces caution against claims of a universal, directly observable micro-wormhole.

## 6. Claim E-05 — Does entanglement conservation imply a wormhole area theorem?

**Primary source:** Ning Bao, Grant N. Remmen, and Jason Pollack, “Entanglement conservation, ER=EPR, and a new classical area theorem for wormholes,” *Journal of High Energy Physics* 07 (2016) 048: https://arxiv.org/abs/1604.08217

This paper develops a specific classical gravitational area theorem for a collection of dynamical wormholes and black holes under stated geometric and energy conditions. The authors interpret it as a gravitational counterpart to entanglement conservation in the ER=EPR setting.

**Boundary:** This is a theorem within a specified classical gravitational setup and its assumptions. It does not establish that every quantum entangled pair has a wormhole, nor that separating two ordinary particles stretches a wormhole or stores energy. A result about maximin areas of specified horizons cannot be generalized to arbitrary entangled laboratory systems without an additional derivation.

**Disposition:** PASS within the theorem's stated hypotheses; FAIL as support for an unrestricted particle-separation/wormhole-stretching story.

## 7. Claim E-06 — Traversability and causal communication

**Relevant primary source:** Ping Gao, Daniel Louis Jafferis, and Aron C. Wall, “Traversable Wormholes via a Double Trace Deformation,” *Journal of High Energy Physics* 12 (2017) 151: https://arxiv.org/abs/1608.05687

This work constructs traversability in a particular AdS black-hole setup using a specified coupling between the two boundaries, producing negative averaged null energy in the relevant setting. It does not imply that the original Einstein-Rosen bridge is traversable, or that ordinary entanglement alone enables faster-than-light communication.

The model illustrates that traversability depends on additional physical structure and carefully specified conditions. It does not provide a general mechanism by which two parties can use entanglement to send controllable superluminal messages.

**Disposition:** PASS for a model-specific traversable-wormhole construction; FAIL for generalizing it to generic entangled particles or ordinary EPR pairs.

## 10. GUA-W comparison boundary

The scientifically defensible observation is that quantum entanglement describes nonseparable correlations in a joint quantum state, while ER=EPR explores whether certain entanglement structures admit a gravitational bridge description. These are relations internal to quantum theory and specified gravitational models.

A GUA-W comparison may ask whether the distinction between a relation and its representation is illuminating. It must not conclude that:
- entanglement is the same thing as GUA-W Joining;
- every joined or correlated system has a wormhole;
- a wormhole is a physical witness in the theological sense;
- ER=EPR establishes the completed architecture;
- Bell nonlocality demonstrates superluminal causal influence.

The completed GUA-W architecture is not altered by this comparison. The possible analogy remains subordinate, bounded, and open to rejection if it fails any Triple Constraint.
