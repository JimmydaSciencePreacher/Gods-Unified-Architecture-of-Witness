## 2. Shared findings

The two audits agree on the following central boundaries:

- The Bekenstein-Hawking relation associates black-hole thermodynamic entropy with horizon area. It does not, by itself, establish a literal local storage screen or a universal information-capacity formula for every arbitrary region.
- AdS/CFT is a powerful and specific theoretical correspondence, not a demonstrated universal dual description for every spacetime or every astrophysical black hole.
- Unitarity of the boundary theory strongly constrains the complete dual gravitational description within the correspondence's scope. It should not be inflated into a complete account of every physical black-hole information question.
- Bulk/boundary encoding is not adequately described as ordinary local bits copied onto an event horizon.
- The Page curve is important in the unitary evaporation problem, and modern gravitational calculations reproduce Page-curve behavior in controlled models. Page time is not universally an exact halfway point in elapsed evaporation time, and the curve need not be symmetric.
- Ryu-Takayanagi-type relations apply in specified holographic settings and approximations. The relevant minimal or extremal surface is not automatically an event horizon or wormhole throat.
- These results do not independently prove a GUA-W axiom or establish a shared physical mechanism with GUA-W.
- The physics audits do not themselves settle Biblical compliance; that requires disciplined Scriptural examination rather than an inference from physics.
